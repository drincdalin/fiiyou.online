Accessibility

Last updated: 05.11.2026

We want fiiyou to be usable by everyone, including people who use a keyboard instead of a mouse, a screen reader, voice control, a very small or very large screen, or who need less motion. Our aim is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What we have done

  • Everything can be used with a keyboard. The current position is always visible, and "Skip to content" is the first thing a keyboard user reaches.
  • Pages have a main area, a navigation area and a heading structure. Buttons, links and fields have names a screen reader can read; the visible label is part of the name, so voice control works.
  • Pop-ups (photo viewer, message options, sharing, reporting) move focus inside, keep it there, close with the Escape key, and give focus back when they close.
  • Text and controls keep a readable contrast in the default look. The site works when zoomed to 200% and does not block pinch-zoom.
  • Animations stop if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Touch targets are large enough for fingers, and gestures (swipe, double tap) always have a normal button as an alternative.
  • We test the main screens with an automated checker (axe), on a phone and on a computer, and fix what it finds.

What is not perfect yet

  • Photos that people upload cannot have a text description yet. Screen reader users hear that there is a photo, not what it shows. We plan to add a description field.
  • People can style their own profile pages. A colour choice made by a profile owner can have low contrast; we cannot guarantee that for pages we do not control.
  • The friends map uses third-party map tiles and has not been fully tested with assistive technology.
  • Automated checks find only part of all problems. We have not had an external audit or a test with a panel of disabled people yet.

Tell us

If something does not work for you, write to contact@fiiyou.online and say which page and which device or assistive technology you use. We will read every message and try to fix the problem or offer another way to do what you wanted.

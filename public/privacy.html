Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29.10.2026

fiiyou is a small, independent social network. We collect only what is needed to run it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

Who is responsible

Drinc Dalin, Beiuș, Romania. Contact: contact@fiiyou.online.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and a password hash (handled by our authentication provider). If you joined through someone's invite link, the username of the person who invited you is kept with your account, only to suggest adding them as a friend.
  • Profile: username, display name, mood, locality, theme and style settings, music link or audio file, cover image, avatar, background, stickers, photos and captions.
  • Activity: posts, comments, likes, guestbook entries, friend requests and friendships, your Top friends, places you add, blocks and reports you file.
  • Messages: direct messages between friends. They are stored in our database and are not end-to-end encrypted; the operator can technically read them.
  • Profile views: a daily count of how many different people viewed a profile. We store which account viewed which profile for that count, but nobody can see this list in the app.

Who can see it

Everything requires a login. Profiles, posts and photos are visible to other signed-in members unless you choose "friends only" where offered. Blocked people cannot see you. Your email is never shown to other members.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Vercel serves the website files and may log visitor IP addresses.
  • OpenStreetMap provides map tiles (your browser requests them, so OpenStreetMap sees your IP address). When you press Search on the map, your search text is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service.
  • Push notification services (Google, Apple or Mozilla, depending on your browser) are used only if you turn on push notifications. Your device's push address is stored in our database and sent to that service to deliver each notification.
  • YouTube is contacted only if you press play on a YouTube music link, using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com player.

Your browser

Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage. This is strictly necessary for the service to work; we do not use it for tracking. Your browser also keeps a short log of recent sign-in events (for example "signed in", "signed out") on your device only, to help diagnose unexpected sign-outs; it is never sent to us.

Where data is processed

Our providers (Supabase, Vercel and the push services) may process data in or outside the European Union. We use these providers because they are needed to run the service, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Your rights

You can export your data (account menu → Export all my data) and delete your account and its content at any time (account menu → Delete account). Uploaded files are removed from storage when they are replaced or deleted. Copies may persist in provider backups for a limited time set by the provider's backup schedule. You can also ask us to access, correct or erase your data by email, and you have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Romania: ANSPDCP).

Age

fiiyou is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will say so on the sign-in page.

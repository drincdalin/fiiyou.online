# Invite Week — announcement draft

Post this as an **Event** in the app. Change the dates and prizes before publishing.

**Title:** Invite Week: bring your people

**Starts:** Monday 23 November 2026, 20:00
**Where:** fiiyou.online

**Body:**

This week is Invite Week.

Your personal invite link is in **Menu → Invite friends**. Send it to people who would actually use fiiyou. When they sign up and confirm their email, they count for you.

The contest runs from Monday 23 November, 00:00, to Sunday 29 November, 23:59, Romania time. Only confirmed new accounts count. Clicks do not. One account per person; duplicate or fake accounts are removed.

I will post the standings here each evening. On Monday I will announce the three people with the most confirmed invites.

What you can win:

1st: a custom profile theme made with you, a printed A4 poster of your profile, and a €15 voucher for a local bookshop or coffee shop.
2nd: a custom sticker pack and a €10 voucher.
3rd: a custom sticker pack.

Everyone who brings at least three confirmed people also gets a sticker pack.

Tie-break: most confirmed sign-ups; if still tied, the most link visits; if still tied, whoever got there first.

To join, open your invite link and send it. That is it. If you cannot find the link, open **Menu → Invite friends**.

- Their profile featured in the announcement for a week.
- A printed A5/A4 poster of their profile page, handed over locally.
- First pick of the next feature added to fiiyou, and their name in the release note.
- A hand-written thank-you card, if the audience is local.

## Contest rules in the dashboard

The dashboard **Invites** tab uses the same week as this announcement:

- Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59, Europe/Bucharest.
- **Sign-ups** are confirmed accounts created through a personal link.
- **Visits** are one browser per inviter per week; they are shown for context but do not decide the winner.
- The table is ordered by confirmed sign-ups this week, then visits, then all-time sign-ups.
